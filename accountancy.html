Overview

Financial records you can make decisions with

Most finance problems we are called in to fix are not complicated. They are late: receipts in a shoebox, a filing deadline already passed, a bank balance nobody can reconcile.

Our accountancy division puts a routine around that. Transactions are recorded on a schedule, management accounts arrive monthly instead of annually, and statutory obligations are tracked on a calendar you can see. When an auditor, a lender or a donor asks a question, the answer is already in the file.

We work with businesses that have no internal accountant at all, and alongside finance teams that need an extra pair of hands at year end.

Questions

Accountancy — frequently asked

Do you work with very small businesses?

Yes. A single-owner trading business with a few dozen transactions a month is a normal engagement for us, and it is usually the point at which good records are cheapest to establish.

Our books are months behind. Is that a problem?

It is common, and it is fixable. We quote catch-up work separately from the ongoing monthly service so you can see exactly what the backlog costs to clear before committing.

Can you take over from our current accountant mid-year?

Yes. We request the trial balance, ledgers and last filed return, reconcile the opening position and continue from there. A mid-year handover does not require you to restart the financial year.

What records do we need to give you?

Typically bank statements, sales invoices, purchase invoices and receipts, payroll details and any prior year statements or returns. We send a short checklist with the quotation so nothing is guessed at.

Do you provide the actual statutory audit?

No — an external auditor must be independent of whoever prepares the accounts. We prepare your records and schedules to the standard an auditor expects, then work with the auditor you appoint.
