Frequently asked

Before you get in touch

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day for written enquiries. Phone calls during business hours are answered immediately or returned the same day.

Do you charge for an initial consultation?

No. The first conversation — understanding your situation and telling you what is involved — is free. You receive a written quotation before any chargeable work starts.

Can I use more than one division?

Yes, and many clients do. Where you engage two or more divisions, we assign a single account manager so you brief once and receive consolidated updates and billing.

Do you work with clients outside Liberia?

Yes. We regularly act for diaspora clients — handling accounts, clearing shipments, arranging travel or supporting a family member’s studies — entirely by phone, WhatsApp and email.

How do you handle my documents?

Financial records, passports, transcripts and commercial documents are seen only by the people working on your file. Originals are returned on completion, and we confirm in writing what we hold and for how long.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Whatever makes the job concrete: for accountancy, the period and what records exist; for travel, dates, travellers and destinations; for education, results, budget and intended course; for logistics, what the goods are, their value and where they are now.
