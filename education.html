Division 03 — Education & training

Honest advice first, then a strong application.

Study placement, admissions support, scholarship research and professional training — for students heading abroad and for organisations developing the people they already have.

Request a quotation (+231) 0880832316

Overview

The right course matters more than the fastest offer

Choosing where to study is one of the largest financial decisions a family makes. It deserves advice that starts with your results, your budget and your intended career — not with whichever institution pays the largest commission.

We counsel on realistic options across countries and course levels, tell you plainly when a plan is unaffordable or unlikely to be admitted, and then do the unglamorous work properly: transcripts, references, personal statements, deadlines and document consistency.

For employers, the same division delivers short professional courses — bookkeeping, office software, customer service, logistics and supervisory skills — scheduled around working hours.

Questions

Education — frequently asked

Do you guarantee admission or a student visa?

No. Admission is decided by the institution and the visa by the immigration authority, and any agent promising either is misleading you. Our work is to make your application as strong, complete and consistent as the facts allow.

Which countries do you advise on?

We advise on the destinations that are realistic for Liberian applicants given cost, entry requirements and visa practicality, and we are explicit about which are competitive for your profile. Where a destination is a poor fit, we say so rather than take the fee.

How much does placement support cost?

Counselling and application support are quoted in writing before we begin, and separately from any third-party charges such as application fees, tests, authentication or tuition deposits. You will always know which payments come to us and which do not.

Can you help if the deadline is close?

Sometimes — it depends on the intake and how complete your documents are. We will tell you honestly whether the deadline is achievable or whether targeting the next intake gives you a materially better application.

Do you run training for companies?

Yes. Short courses can be delivered for a team, scheduled around working hours, and tailored to the systems your staff actually use.
