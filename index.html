Why a holding company

The work most businesses juggle, consolidated

A growing organisation rarely needs just one service. It needs the tax filing done, the director flown out, the graduate trainee placed and the consignment cleared — usually in the same month.

Symmetrix exists so that work sits with one accountable partner instead of four unconnected vendors. Your accountant already knows the shipment’s duty cost. Your travel desk already knows the training dates. Nothing gets explained twice, and nothing falls between the gaps.

  • One point of contact across every service you use with us.
  • Written quotations before work starts — scope, price and timeline in writing.
  • Consolidated records, so the finance trail of every trip and shipment is already in your books.

Our standards

What every client gets, in every division

These are not aspirations. They are the conditions we hold our own teams to, and the basis on which you should judge us.

Everything in writing

Scope, fees and deadlines are confirmed in a written quotation before any work begins. No surprises on the invoice.

Confidentiality by default

Financial records, passports and personal documents are handled on a need-to-know basis and returned or destroyed on request.

A named person

You deal with someone who knows your file — not a queue. Escalation goes to management, not to voicemail.

Advice before sales

If a cheaper route, a different country or a simpler structure serves you better, we will say so — including when it earns us less.

Compliance first

Filings, customs declarations and admissions paperwork are prepared to survive scrutiny, because shortcuts cost clients more later.

Dates we can keep

We quote realistic timelines and flag slippage the moment we see it, rather than the day after a deadline passes.
