Travel arranged properly — and fixed when plans change.

Air ticketing, visa documentation, accommodation and corporate travel management for individuals, families, delegations and companies travelling out of Liberia or in.

Request a quotation (+231) 0880832316

Overview

The difference is what happens when something goes wrong

Anyone can quote a fare. The value of a travel agent shows at 11pm when a connection is cancelled, a visa appointment moves or a passenger is told at check-in that a document is missing.

Our travels division books carefully in the first place — sensible connection times, correct name spellings against the passport, fare rules explained before you pay — and stays reachable afterwards. Reissues, refunds and rerouting are handled by the same person who made the booking.

For companies, we also run travel as a managed service: approved routes, spend visibility and a single monthly statement that reconciles against your accounts.

Process

Booked once, booked correctly

We confirm details before money changes hands, because a misspelled name or an impossible connection is far more expensive to fix later.

Tell us the trip

Dates, destinations, travellers, budget and any fixed commitments at either end.

Options in writing

Two or three routings with total costs and the fare conditions that matter, so you choose knowingly.

Confirm & issue

Details verified against passports, payment confirmed, tickets and reservations issued the same day where possible.

Travel support

Documents checked before departure, and a reachable contact for changes while you are on the road.

Questions

Travels — frequently asked

Can you guarantee a visa will be approved?

No, and no honest agent can — the decision belongs to the embassy or consulate alone. What we can do is make sure your application is complete, consistent and submitted with the documents that destination requires, which is where most avoidable refusals come from.

My flight was cancelled. Can you help if you did not book it?

We will always try, but our ability to reissue or reroute is far greater on tickets we issued ourselves. If you booked elsewhere, contact the issuing agent or airline first — then call us if you are stuck.

Do you handle travel for groups?

Yes. Delegations, church and school groups, conference parties and training cohorts are routine. Group bookings often unlock better fares and deposit terms than individual tickets.

What do you need to issue a ticket?

Passport bio-data page for each traveller (exact name, number and expiry), dates and destinations, contact phone and email, and confirmation of the fare you have selected.

Can you bill our company monthly instead of per trip?

Yes, under a corporate travel agreement with agreed credit terms. This is also where our accountancy division helps: travel spend arrives already coded for your books.
